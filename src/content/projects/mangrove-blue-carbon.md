This project was produced during an **International Organization Internship** with Morobe Development Foundation Inc. through the **UNDP-administered United Nations Volunteers (UNV)** platform.

The United Nations Volunteers (UNV) programme is administered by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP).

![Conceptual model of mangrove carbon dynamics](/figures/mangrove-carbon-dynamics.png)

The review manuscript synthesizes literature on:

- mangrove carbon sequestration and blue carbon policy;
- sea-level rise and coastal squeeze;
- nutrient loading from local anthropogenic stressors;
- national-scale climate mitigation potential;
- measurement limitations and future monitoring needs.

For the homepage, this experience broadens the profile beyond AI research and shows scientific writing, literature synthesis, and international research collaboration.
