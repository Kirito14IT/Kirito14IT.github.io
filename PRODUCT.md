# Carey academic website

A bilingual academic homepage for Carey (Zhihua Wang / 王志华). Readers include researchers, collaborators, recruiters, and people exploring the author's publications and open-source work.

The public site provides a concise introduction, dated news, publications, education, projects, research and work experience, awards, academic service, a CV page, and technical notes. English and Chinese routes retain their language switch and matching canonical/alternate links.

Readers should be able to scan research work, follow source links, open or download current CV PDFs, and contact the author quickly. Publications preserve authorship marks, venue/status information, resource links, and homepage-specific author/status/rating choices. CV PDF URLs, hash-based versions, filenames, and the detailed version's lazy preview remain functional.

The approved visual direction follows the compact academic character of xuyanye.com: a white page, system sans-serif text, blue links, thin rules, and content rows. The sidebar carries identity, school, city, CV, and seven contact links; it becomes a compact header on small screens. The shared style applies to public BaseLayout pages, excluding the private internship tracker and the standalone XSafeClaw public application.

The assistant, click feedback, optional meteor effects, visitor statistics, and animated project demonstrations remain supported. Motion-sensitive readers receive reduced-motion behavior; animated demonstrations loop continuously and provide a static fallback for reduced motion.
